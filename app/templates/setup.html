4 Put it on your lock screen

{% if not garden %}

Your lock-screen link appears here once the garden is set.

{% else %}

Optional. On an iPhone, a Shortcuts automation can set each morning's plan as your lock screen, so you see it without opening anything.

  1. Open this page on your iPhone and copy the link above.
  2. In the Shortcuts app, go to Automation, tap New Automation, then Time of Day. Pick a few minutes after {{ morning_hour }}:00, Daily, and Run Immediately.
  3. Add the action Get Contents of URL and paste the link.
  4. Add Set Wallpaper Photo, choose your lock screen, and turn off Show Preview and Crop to Subject.

Action names can differ a little between iOS versions. Your iPhone must be able to reach Tendril when the automation runs, for example on the same Wi-Fi. On Android, an automation app such as MacroDroid can do the same with a "set wallpaper from URL" action.
